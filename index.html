Research

Our research focuses on translational approaches to head and neck oncology.

View all

Nasopharyngeal Carcinoma Screening

Developing and validating early detection strategies for NPC in high-risk populations using EBV biomarkers and cell-free DNA.

Tumor Immunology

Investigating the tumor immune microenvironment in head and neck cancers, with focus on innate lymphoid cells and immunotherapy approaches.

Digital Health Technologies

Innovating diagnostic tools and technologies, including 3D-printed medical devices and digital detection methods for infectious diseases.
